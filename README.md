## Motivation for Construction
When it comes to designing projects like these, it's all about discovery and learning new processes to determine intuitive answers to problems around us. I was motivated by a strong willingness to learn industry standard software — SolidWorks, OpenRocket, and KiCad — alongside physical processes — soldering, product assembly, and calculations to determine aerodynamics.
